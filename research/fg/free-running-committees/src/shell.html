Free-running FG committees

One committee every 4 seconds, and rounds of 23 units that are allowed to slide across slot boundaries. Committees then rotate through the slot on their own, every validator votes exactly every 92 s, validators can pick their own seat, and the round becomes pure accounting. The appendix asks where each FG design picks its target and what that does to time-to-finality.
