Reading it. The ideal model has no aggregation or inclusion delay, everyone is online, and time runs in 92 s rounds. Each row shows which votes count for which checkpoint, and tracks one checkpoint from the moment its target is picked (◆) to justification (J) and finalization (F). The red segment is the design's "target picked deep" cost: a sticky voter ignores the entry chosen at the 2/3 quorum until its next round, so the target waits a third of a round before its first vote. In the simulation that wait is 20 s at p = 1 and 48–88 s near 2/3. Moving the pick to the round start removes that wait (row 3); the block lag still spans a whole round because targets are picked once per round. The right column gives the lag range over birth time and its expected value.
