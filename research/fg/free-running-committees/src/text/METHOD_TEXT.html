• Clock lane: sim/clock.py (python3 sim/clock.py all, ~6 s), write-up in results/clock.md.
    • It reuses Q18's simulator for the constant-lag model and reproduces Q18's 0.696 / 0.826 / 1.130 rounds per height exactly.
    • It adds an explicit-time model of §1: a vote cast at t lands in the block at 12·⌈(t + 8)/12⌉ s, and the quorum block becomes visible 1 s after its slot start. The model uses 40·C validators and Q18's random offline sets.
  • Design lane: sim/designs.py (all, ~70 s; traces writes Figure 5's data), write-up in results/designs.md.
    • It is a copy of the prefix-consensus simulator with 92 s rounds and three new engines: sticky voters, round-aligned target, and prefix with sticky voters.
    • At C = 24 it reproduces the prefix study's tables byte for byte. Its ideal-model runs match the closed forms 5/3, 5/2, 13/6, 11/6 and 4/3.
  • Independent check: sim/check_frc.py, about 80 lines by the orchestrating agent. It spreads offline stake evenly over cohorts and reproduces:
    • the vote gaps and the committees per block;
    • the explicit-time seconds per height: 72 / 84 / 96 / 96 / 108 s for the free-running clock at p = 1 / 0.9 / 0.8 / 0.7 / 0.67.
    It agrees with the clock lane at p = 1, 0.9 and 0.67. At 0.8 and 0.7 the clock lane's random offline sets average shorter and longer heights: 92 and 98.2 s.
  • This page: python3 build.py inlines src/ and results/*.json into one file. Plain JavaScript, no libraries.
