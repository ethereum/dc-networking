Reading it. The hand is the active committee index, ⌊t / 4 s⌋ mod 23. It moves one seat every 4 s and completes a lap every 92 s. The outer track marks where slots begin during the current lap; it moves by one seat per lap and repeats every three laps. Bars show each seat's stake by who chose it, against the per-seat cap (dashed circle). Click a seat (or focus it and press Enter) to make it yours; the table lists its next four votes. The four presets illustrate the cases discussed below; their stakes are made up for the picture, not simulated.
