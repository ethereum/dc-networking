Reading it. Each lane is one design over the same six rounds. The top row has one cell per 4 s unit, coloured by the checkpoint its votes count for: alternate heights are shaded alternately, and hatched cells are stale votes for a height that already closed. The row below is the chain, one block per slot, coloured by its state at the playhead: available, justified, finalized. A ◇ marks each checkpoint's target block once it is picked. Hover anything for exact times. "Finalized head is … s old" is the depth of finality at the playhead.
