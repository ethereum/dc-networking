Reading it. Rows are rounds; columns are the 24 four-second units of eight slots, shaded by in-slot offset. Click any cell to trace that committee in time order; the strip below gives its absolute vote times under both schedules. Free-running, slot view draws the proposal on the same grid: the idle column is gone, and a thick bar marks where each 92 s round begins, one unit earlier per row. Round view aligns rows by round instead: the committee order never changes, and the slot boundaries (thick ticks) slide under it.
