# PQ Gasper: the current spec

## Short version

- **What it is.** An emergency fork that replaces BLS with leanXMSS and keeps Gasper ([beacon-chain.md:153–160](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L153-L160)). It builds on EIP-8483, the key-registration fork, which builds on Gloas ([:233–234](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L233-L234)). Slots stay at 12 s.
- **Attestations.** 16 committees per slot on 16 subnets, one committee per subnet. Each committee has an aggregation committee of 4, sampled by effective balance and public one epoch ahead. Each aggregate carries its own STARK, and the next proposer merges them into one `body.proof`.
- **Gone.** Sync committees and light clients, payload attestations in blocks, selection proofs, BLS RANDAO, deposit signatures, and the `voluntary_exit` and `bls_to_execution_change` topics.
- **Builders.** They sign bids and envelopes with XMSS. The spec gives them no role in attestations.

## 1. Keys and signatures

- **leanXMSS.**
  - A signature is 2,404 B: the leaf index, then one signature per layer ([beacon-chain.md:282–291](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L282-L291), [:385](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L385)). A public key is 32 B ([:273–280](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L273-L280)).
  - It is stateful: signing two messages at one leaf breaks the key ([:162–172](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L162-L172)). Clients should keep a per-leaf record of signing roots that survives restarts ([validator.md:576–590](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/validator.md#L576-L590)).
- **Leaf budget.** Each message type has its own leaves ([beacon-chain.md:383–405](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L383-L405), [:938–967](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L938-L967), [:1047–1056](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L1047-L1056)):
  - The first 2^15 leaves are reserved for messages without a slot, such as preregistrations and exits. Each is used once.
  - The next 2^29 are attestation leaves, one per target epoch. Every attester of an epoch signs at the same index. A second vote for the same target is a slashable double vote and also exposes the leaf ([:940–942](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L940-L942)).
  - The next 2^30 are bid leaves, used in any order, each once. Verification reads the index from the signature ([:1706–1721](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L1706-L1721)).
  - Everything else takes one of 8 leaves per slot, counted from genesis: 0 block, 1 aggregate, 2 PTC vote, 3 proposer preferences, 4 payload envelope. Offsets 5–7 are unused.
  - With a 2^32-leaf key, the per-slot range lasts ~128 years from genesis.
- **One aggregate per duty.** The aggregate leaf belongs to the slot ([validator.md:537–552](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/validator.md#L537-L552)). An aggregator therefore signs one aggregate per duty, since an improved one would reuse the leaf. Gossip agrees: the first valid aggregate per (target epoch, aggregator) wins ([p2p-interface.md:401–406](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/p2p-interface.md#L401-L406)).
- **RANDAO.** XMSS signatures are not unique, so a signed reveal could be ground ([beacon-chain.md:177–181](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L177-L181)).
  - Each validator registers a VRF root: the root of a depth-31 Merkle tree with one secret per slot ([validator.md:100–108](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/validator.md#L100-L108)).
  - The reveal is the slot's leaf plus its branch, a 1,024 B `VRFProof` ([beacon-chain.md:250–260](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L250-L260), [:1623–1645](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L1623-L1645)). A proposer can withhold its block but cannot grind the reveal.
- **STARK interface** ([beacon-chain.md:869–890](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L869-L890)):
  - `ProveAggregates`, `MergeProofs` and `VerifyAggregates` work on statements. A statement is an ordered list of public keys, one leaf index and one message ([:838–846](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L838-L846)).
  - Aggregates and blocks list keys in committee order ([:1256–1287](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L1256-L1287)). An `IndexedAttestation` lists them by sorted validator index ([:1111–1140](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L1111-L1140)).
  - The reference implementation concatenates signatures. It stands in for a recursive STARK ([:887–890](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L887-L890)).

## 2. The attestation path

### Committees and subnets

- **16 of each.** `MAX_COMMITTEES_PER_SLOT_PQ = 16` ([beacon-chain.md:434](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L434), [:1194–1211](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L1194-L1211)) and `ATTESTATION_SUBNET_COUNT_PQ = 16` ([p2p-interface.md:49–53](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/p2p-interface.md#L49-L53)). The commit wants "as many attestation committees per slot as there are attestation subnets" ([`1b246ff`](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/commit/1b246ff72126eeb3993d100acc851372b3798600)). Committee *i* of a slot always lands on subnet *i* ([validator.md:460–474](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/validator.md#L460-L474)).
- **Committee size** is V/512 once V exceeds 65,536: 1,400 at V = 716,800 (the leanVM benchmark), 1,563 at 800,000, and 1,953 at 1,000,000.
- **Backbone.** `SUBNETS_PER_NODE = 2` is unchanged ([mainnet.yaml:194](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/configs/mainnet.yaml#L194)). A node now holds 2 of 16 subnets, i.e. 1/8 of all attestation traffic instead of 1/32 ([p2p-interface.md:227–255](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/p2p-interface.md#L227-L255)). `attnets` keeps 64 bits, and only the first 16 may be set ([:875–878](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/p2p-interface.md#L875-L878)).
- **Duty subscription** is unchanged. An aggregator subscribes to its subnet, and other attesters only need peers on it ([phase0/validator.md:353–359](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/phase0/validator.md#L353-L359)).

### Single attestations

- A `SingleAttestation` is 2,548 B, against 240 B with BLS ([beacon-chain.md:735–744](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L735-L744)). It is signed at the target-epoch leaf ([validator.md:441–456](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/validator.md#L441-L456)).
- It goes out when the block arrives or at `ATTESTATION_DUE_BPS_GLOAS` = 25 % (3 s), whichever is first ([phase0/validator.md:610–614](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/phase0/validator.md#L610-L614), [mainnet.yaml:106](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/configs/mainnet.yaml#L106)).
- Gossip accepts one per (target epoch, validator), on the right subnet, from a committee member, with a valid XMSS signature ([p2p-interface.md:898–957](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/p2p-interface.md#L898-L957)).

### Aggregation committees

- **Size.** Exactly `AGGREGATORS_PER_COMMITTEE = 4` distinct validators per beacon committee ([beacon-chain.md:377–379](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L377-L379)), so 64 aggregators per slot.
- **Sampling.** They are drawn from the beacon committee by effective balance, without replacement. The seed is `get_seed(state, epoch, DOMAIN_SELECTION_PROOF)` plus slot and committee index ([:969–996](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L969-L996), [gloas/beacon-chain.md:1198–1230](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/gloas/beacon-chain.md#L1198-L1230)). Per draw, a 2,048 ETH validator is 64× as likely as a 32 ETH one.
- **Lookahead.** `aggregation_committee_window` caches the previous, current and next epoch ([beacon-chain.md:361–371](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L361-L371)). At the end of epoch E, `process_aggregation_committee_window` fills epoch E+2 ([:1457–1476](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L1457-L1476)). Epoch E's aggregators are therefore public from the first slot of E−1, 32–64 slots ahead, drawn from the same RANDAO mix as the committees.
- **No selection proof.** `is_aggregator` is a lookup ([validator.md:478–495](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/validator.md#L478-L495)), and `AggregateAndProof` drops `selection_proof` ([:75–85](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/validator.md#L75-L85)). The commit: "Every committee now has exactly four aggregators rather than a random number that is sometimes zero" ([`e26cc6f`](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/commit/e26cc6fe90416184d3f4369cbc49e13ebe9ad411)).

### Aggregates on the wire

- **Build.** Each aggregator proves its committee's signatures in committee order ([validator.md:497–512](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/validator.md#L497-L512)). It broadcasts at `AGGREGATE_DUE_BPS_GLOAS` = 50 % (6 s) ([phase0/validator.md:776–779](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/phase0/validator.md#L776-L779), [mainnet.yaml:108](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/configs/mainnet.yaml#L108)).
- **Size.** `SignedAggregateAndProof` holds the aggregator index, an unsigned `Attestation`, the `proof` and an XMSS signature ([validator.md:75–94](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/validator.md#L75-L94)). With 1,400 attesters and leanVM's 209 KiB proof, it is ≈ 212 KiB. The gossip bound of 1,055,231 B follows from the 1 MiB proof placeholder ([p2p-interface.md:65–69](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/p2p-interface.md#L65-L69)).
- **Validation** of `beacon_aggregate_and_proof` ([p2p-interface.md:373–512](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/p2p-interface.md#L373-L512)), in this order:
  - Exactly one committee ([:395–399](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/p2p-interface.md#L395-L399)), and only the first aggregate per (target epoch, aggregator) ([:401–406](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/p2p-interface.md#L401-L406)).
  - **During its slot**, IGNORE unless its attesting balance beats the best seen for (data, committee) ([:455–463](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/p2p-interface.md#L455-L463), [`2c5edd4`](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/commit/2c5edd4ae4983de4d2ea474e035aece5d7e3bb9f)).
  - **After its slot**, IGNORE only if a seen aggregate covers all its attesters ([:464–468](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/p2p-interface.md#L464-L468), [`ee867e9`](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/commit/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129)).
  - REJECT unless the sender is in the aggregation committee ([:470–473](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/p2p-interface.md#L470-L473)). Then check its XMSS signature ([:475–482](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/p2p-interface.md#L475-L482)) and the STARK ([:484–488](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/p2p-interface.md#L484-L488)).
- **Why two regimes** (`ee867e9`). During the slot, no vote can be on chain yet, so more balance means more new votes. After it, some votes may already be on chain on one fork, so balance could be padded with them. The superset rule "never drops a vote".
- **Effect.** During the slot, a node forwards an aggregate only if it improves on the best one seen for its data and committee. Each node thus forwards 1–4 per committee and data, not always 4. The cheap balance check runs before the STARK check.

### Into the block

- **On-chain aggregate.** The proposer of slot N+1 merges network aggregates with equal `AttestationData` into one `Attestation`, one aggregate per committee ([validator.md:242–273](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/validator.md#L242-L273)). A slot whose attesters agree fits in one `Attestation`, and a block holds up to `MAX_ATTESTATIONS_PQ = 16` ([beacon-chain.md:450](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L450)).
- **Merged proof.** It sets `body.proof = MergeProofs(proofs)`, ordered by attestation, then committee index ([validator.md:275–280](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/validator.md#L275-L280)). `body.proof` is the block's only evidence of attestation signatures ([beacon-chain.md:227–231](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L227-L231), [:487–513](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L487-L513)). leanVM merges 16 → 1 in 0.37–1.33 s, into 303 KiB.
- **Overlap.** Two overlapping aggregates for one committee cannot be unioned without the raw signatures. The proposer picks one, or spends a second `Attestation` on the other.
- **Block size.** ≈ 313 KiB with one full-slot attestation and a 21-blob bid. About 303 KiB of it is the proof.

### State transition

- `process_attestation` keeps its checks minus the signature: the target epoch is current or previous, the inclusion delay is ≥ 1, and each listed committee has an attester ([beacon-chain.md:1865–1945](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L1865-L1945)).
- `process_stark` builds one statement per (attestation, committee) and asserts `VerifyAggregates(statements, body.proof)` ([:1819–1827](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L1819-L1827)). It runs after all attestations ([:1806–1810](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L1806-L1810)).
- Nothing checks who aggregated. `Attestation` has no aggregator field ([:474–485](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L474-L485)), and the proof only has to verify.
- **Credit** is Gloas's: head needs inclusion in the next slot, source within 5 slots, and target any time in the window ([gloas/beacon-chain.md:1379–1384](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/gloas/beacon-chain.md#L1379-L1384)). Same-slot votes also add weight to that slot's builder payment ([beacon-chain.md:1925–1931](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L1925-L1931)).

### Fork choice

- `on_attestation` no longer checks a signature. Gossip checks the signature or `proof` of wire votes, and `process_stark` checks block votes ([fork-choice.md:92–120](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/fork-choice.md#L92-L120)).
- PTC votes reach fork choice only from the wire, for the current slot, with an XMSS check ([fork-choice.md:20–22](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/fork-choice.md#L20-L22), [:122–174](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/fork-choice.md#L122-L174)). `on_block` drops `notify_ptc_messages` ([:79–80](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/fork-choice.md#L79-L80)).

## 3. Slot timeline

12 s slots with Gloas's deadlines ([gloas/validator.md:41–48](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/gloas/validator.md#L41-L48), [mainnet.yaml:106–116](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/configs/mainnet.yaml#L106-L116)). V = 716,800, so 1,400 per committee.

| t (s) | Deadline | Event | Bytes per slot, one copy | Compute |
|---:|---|---|---:|---:|
| 0 | slot start | block N, with `body.proof` for slot N−1 | ≈ 313 KiB | merge before t = 0: 0.37–1.33 s |
| ≤ 3 | `ATTESTATION_DUE_BPS_GLOAS` 2500 | 22,400 `SingleAttestation`s, 1,400 per subnet | 3.6 MB per subnet, 57 MB in all | one XMSS verify per vote |
| 3–6 | — | 4 aggregators per committee prove | — | 0.24–1.12 s per proof |
| 6 | `AGGREGATE_DUE_BPS_GLOAS` 5000 | 16–64 aggregates on the global topic | 3.3–13.2 MiB | one STARK verify per forwarded aggregate |
| 6 | `PAYLOAD_DUE_BPS` 5000 | builder reveals the payload envelope | — | — |
| 9 | `PAYLOAD_ATTESTATION_DUE_BPS` 7500 | ≤ 512 PTC votes on a global topic | 1.2 MiB | — |
| 11.5 | slot end − 500 ms clock disparity | aggregate gossip switches to the superset rule | — | — |
| 12 | slot N+1 | proposer N+1 publishes | ≈ 313 KiB | — |

- **The proposer's window** is 6 s, from the aggregate deadline to its slot. In it, aggregates must propagate and be verified, and the proposer must merge them (0.37–1.33 s). Its ~313 KiB block must then reach attesters before 3 s.
- **A node on 2 subnets** receives ≈ 7.1 MB of single attestations per slot (8.0 MB at V = 800,000) before gossip duplicates. Our runs counted 4–5 copies ([../pq/README.md](../pq/README.md)).
- **EIP-8198** ("Quick Slots", 10 s) is a separate feature on Heze, and PQ does not build on it ([eip8198/beacon-chain.md:36](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/eip8198/beacon-chain.md#L36), [mainnet.yaml:124](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/configs/mainnet.yaml#L124)). On 10 s slots the same basis points give 2.5 / 5 / 5 / 7.5 s, and the proposer's window shrinks to 5 s.
- **History.** At ~303 KiB per block, proofs add ~2.2 GB per day. Gloas's serving window of 14,299 epochs holds ~140 GB of them ([gloas/p2p-interface.md:256–263](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/gloas/p2p-interface.md#L256-L263)).

## 4. What was removed, and the builder side

- **Sync committees** go, with the light-client protocol and its topics ([beacon-chain.md:157–160](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L157-L160), [p2p-interface.md:44–45](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/p2p-interface.md#L44-L45)). Issuance drops by the sync reward weight, 2/64 ([beacon-chain.md:1604–1621](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L1604-L1621)).
- **Payload attestations leave the block**, so proposers no longer aggregate them ([:223–225](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L223-L225), [validator.md:300–303](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/validator.md#L300-L303)). The PTC still votes on its global topic: up to 512 × 2,454 B ≈ 1.2 MiB per slot, against 75 KB with BLS ([p2p-interface.md:583–648](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/p2p-interface.md#L583-L648), [gloas/beacon-chain.md:600](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/gloas/beacon-chain.md#L600)).
- **Selection proofs** and `get_slot_signature` go ([validator.md:478–495](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/validator.md#L478-L495)). So does **BLS RANDAO**: `get_epoch_signature` is deprecated in favour of the VRF tree ([:236–240](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/validator.md#L236-L240)).
- **Exits and credentials.** Voluntary exits become EL requests, and the `voluntary_exit` topic and body field go ([beacon-chain.md:216–219](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L216-L219), [p2p-interface.md:514–517](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/p2p-interface.md#L514-L517)). `BLSToExecutionChange` is disabled ([beacon-chain.md:221](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L221)). Deposits carry no signature; EIP-8205-style preregistrations protect them ([:195–214](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L195-L214)).

### Builders

- **Bids.** A builder signs each bid at a fresh leaf from the 2^30 bid range ([builder.md:28–32](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/builder.md#L28-L32), [beacon-chain.md:1706–1721](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L1706-L1721)).
  - Why ([`4af3dd8`](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/commit/4af3dd8ca0508b895aa9544e1f2abcaea4bd61dd)): a per-slot leaf would forbid raising a bid or bidding on two parents. Gloas allows one bid per staked ETH per (slot, parent) ([gloas/p2p-interface.md:348–352](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/gloas/p2p-interface.md#L348-L352)).
  - A builder out of bid leaves exits and rejoins with a new key.
- **Envelopes** use the slot's envelope leaf. If a proposer puts the bid in two blocks, the builder signs an envelope for at most one ([builder.md:103–124](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/builder.md#L103-L124)).
- **Self-builds** carry an all-zero signature ([beacon-chain.md:1734–1737](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L1734-L1737)).
- **Why `MAX_SIGNED_EXECUTION_PAYLOAD_BID_SIZE_PQ` is 199,240 B** ([p2p-interface.md:69](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/p2p-interface.md#L69)). It is Gloas's 196,932 B bound ([gloas/p2p-interface.md:79](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/gloas/p2p-interface.md#L79)) with the 96 B BLS signature swapped for 2,404 B. The worst case of 4,096 KZG commitments × 48 B = 196,608 B sets the bound, not the signature. A 21-blob bid is ≈ 3.6 KB.
- **Attestations.** Builders have no attestation duty. The one coupling is Gloas's payment rule:
  - A delivered payload settles the bid directly ([gloas/beacon-chain.md:1797–1804](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/gloas/beacon-chain.md#L1797-L1804)).
  - A withheld payload is still paid only if same-slot votes for the block reach 60 % of a slot's stake ([beacon-chain.md:1925–1931](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L1925-L1931), [gloas/beacon-chain.md:1433–1439](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/gloas/beacon-chain.md#L1433-L1439), [:1719–1731](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/gloas/beacon-chain.md#L1719-L1731)).
  - So whoever controls the inclusion of same-slot votes also decides whether a withholding builder pays.

## 5. Placeholders and open items

- **STARKs are stubs.** The reference `ProveAggregates` and `MergeProofs` concatenate signatures ([beacon-chain.md:887–890](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L887-L890)). `MAX_STARK_PROOF_SIZE = 1 MiB` is a placeholder, and the stub fits 436 signatures, fewer than one committee ([:440–444](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L440-L444)).
- **Verification cost is unspecified.** Nodes check a STARK per forwarded aggregate ([p2p-interface.md:484–488](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/p2p-interface.md#L484-L488)) and the block's proof on import ([beacon-chain.md:1819–1827](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L1819-L1827)). The spec gives no cost. Anton Nashatyrev measured 9.6–12.6 ms to verify a merged proof on an M3 Max ([pq-gasper.md](https://github.com/Nashatyrev/jvm-libp2p/blob/pq-gasper/pq-gasper.md), leanVM `e723a8c`).
- **Slashing evidence.** An `IndexedAttestation` proves keys in sorted index order ([beacon-chain.md:1111–1140](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L1111-L1140)), while aggregates and blocks prove committee order. Evidence built from an observed aggregate therefore needs a fresh proof. The bound of 2,048 × 16 = 32,768 indices ([:1128](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/beacon-chain.md#L1128)) is below a full slot once V > 1,048,576.
- **Fork.** `PQ_FORK_EPOCH` is TBD and the config "not definitive" ([fork.md:85–92](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/fork.md#L85-L92)). Deposit theft through registrations is an open TODO ([:76–83](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/fork.md#L76-L83)). Unregistered validators exit at the fork and become withdrawable 2^14 epochs later ([:34–44](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/ee867e912a7c4c47229e08332db7c5c9c4f4b129/specs/_features/pq/fork.md#L34-L44)).
- **In-slot displacement (our reading, not in the spec).**
  - During the slot, a node drops an honest aggregate if it already saw one with at least as much balance.
  - An aggregator that also controls validators in the committee can keep their votes off the subnet. It can then leave out an equal balance of honest votes and still win on balance.
  - Each honest aggregator signs once, so it cannot answer with a new aggregate. The left-out votes then depend on the gossip race, or on someone re-proving them from the subnet.

## Against our notes

- **[aggregator-selection.md](aggregator-selection.md): the draw matches.** Its "draw-4" is what landed: exactly 4 per committee, one committee per subnet per slot, public, from the committee's RANDAO mix, known 32–64 slots ahead. Three differences:
  - The spec samples by effective balance. Seats follow stake, while the note's machine counts use validators per machine.
  - There is no fallback. Non-members are REJECTed on `beacon_aggregate_and_proof`, and each aggregator gets one aggregate per epoch. The note's mitigation 1 (a permissionless fallback) needs a spec change.
  - The note's 13 MiB per slot (64 × 209 KiB) is now an upper bound, because of the in-slot balance rule.
- **The private lottery did not need a new PQ VRF.**
  - The first PQ commit ([`4dabe73`](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/commit/4dabe73aec6cd75cfb7eaf03a8ccf89fbb22ffe9)) selected aggregators privately with the VRF tree. It hashed the slot's leaf with the validator index and the finalized root, targeting 4 per committee ([validator.md:505–525 @ `e93202d`](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/blob/e93202dd262100a25d7efa2ce5c6ac688ae28174/specs/_features/pq/validator.md#L505-L525)).
  - The selection proof was the 1,024 B leaf with its branch. `e26cc6f` replaced the lottery with the public draw to get exactly 4.
  - The note's per-slot Merkle-tree option was thus already in the spec. Keeping privacy costs ~1 KiB per aggregate, not a 116 KB WHIR proof.
- **[../pq/README.md](../pq/README.md).**
  - "One index per 12-second slot" is now 8 per slot, plus one per target epoch for attestations, 2^30 bid leaves and 2^15 reserved ones.
  - All attesters of an epoch share a leaf, which suits leanVM's grouping by (leaf, message).
  - The PQ Gasper row (16 leaf proofs, one recursion level) matches the spec.
- **[README.md](README.md) avenues.** Avenue 9 (fewer aggregators, 16 → 4) landed. Avenue 5 (builders aggregate) has no counterpart in the spec; [builders.md](builders.md) argues for it.

## The `pq-dc` branch

terence's [`pq-dc`](https://github.com/ethereum/consensus-specs/commit/bde2349e1e86763aed845e8a064cb22805628e0c) (2026-11-17) forks from `e26cc6f` and replaces PQ Gasper with decoupled consensus (EIP-8453). Its commit message says "The aggregation load of PQ-Gasper looks infeasible within a slot."
- Attestations carry a `round` instead of an epoch and get one leaf per round. Head votes get a per-slot leaf, and the block's `proof` covers both.
- Beacon committees and aggregation committees are removed.
- Head-vote proofs, multi-slot aggregation and aggregator selection are TODOs.
