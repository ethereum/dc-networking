A PQ Gasper subnet carries 16× the signatures of a subnet in our 16-subnet runs

Signatures per slot that every node in one subnet must take in and forward within 4 s

PQ Gasper sends about 32k signatures per slot, 2,000 in each of 16 subnets. leanVM benchmarks a lighter version, 16 subnets of 1,400 (716,800 validators). Our realistic runs put 2,000 signatures per slot on 16 subnets, 125 each; home nodes joined up to three.
